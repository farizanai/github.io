Fariza Siddiqua

I am a Ph.D. student and Research Associate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at The University of Texas at El Paso, advised by Dr. Md Maruf Hossain Shuvo.

My research sits where machine learning meets hardware: making AI models cheaper to run, designing accelerators for them, and using large language models to help design chips. Lately I've been measuring what compression actually saves in joules, not just parameters — see the blog for interactive write-ups.

Before UTEP, I received my B.Sc. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from BUET and worked as a Radio Service Specialist Engineer at Banglalink in Dhaka.
